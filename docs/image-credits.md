All photographs in `public/images/covers/` come from Wikimedia Commons under the licences below. They were downloaded once (2000px renditions), resized to 1920px wide JPEG, and are served locally through `next/image` — nothing is hotlinked at runtime.

They are used as **illustrative scenery** for trip covers and the sign-in page. They are never presented as photos of a specific hotel or booking; the accommodation card labels its image as an illustrative destination photo.
